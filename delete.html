Delete your Kora account and data

Last updated: 13 August 2026

You can delete your Kora account and everything stored with it at any time, either from inside the app or by sending one email. There is no charge and you do not have to give a reason.

The fastest way — inside the app

  1. Open Kora on your phone
  2. Go to Settings
  3. Tap Delete all my data
  4. Confirm when it asks

This removes the copy held on Kora's servers first, and then wipes everything from your phone. Your account is closed and your username is released. It happens immediately.

If you no longer have the app

Email galacticfighter47@gmail.com from any address, with:

  • The subject line Delete my account
  • Your Kora username

Your account and everything stored under it will be deleted within 30 days, and you will get a reply confirming it is done.

Do not send your password or recovery code. They are never needed, and nobody from Kora will ever ask for them.

Nothing is kept afterwards. There is no archive, no backup copy and no anonymised version retained. Kora holds no billing records, because it is free.

Two things to know first

It cannot be undone

There is no recovery window and no backup to restore from. Once it is gone, it is gone — including your entire business history. If you want to keep a record, write down the figures that matter before you delete.

Uninstalling is not the same thing

Removing the app clears your phone but leaves the copy on the server if you have an account. Use Delete all my data before you uninstall, or send the email above.

If you never made an account

Then nothing of yours is on any server at all — everything stayed on your phone. Uninstalling Kora deletes all of it, and there is nothing else to request.

More detail about what Kora stores and why is in the privacy policy.
